Bridging Education & Technology

AcademeForge is a forward-thinking EdTech platform built to remove barriers between learning and technology. We believe powerful digital tools should be accessible to educators, students, and developers everywhere.

The APK Builder is one piece of a larger ecosystem — enabling institutions, creators, and independent developers to wrap their web-based learning tools into mobile-first Android experiences without any coding knowledge.
